# Fandom Frenzy Privacy Policy


Effective Date: [19/2/2026]

Fandom Frenzy ("the Game") is a fan-made word puzzle application created for entertainment purposes. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to any music groups, entertainment companies, drama studios, actors, or celebrities mentioned within the game.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Fandom Frenzy does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

However, the Game uses third-party services that may collect certain information automatically.

2. Google Play Games Services

The Game uses Google Play Games Services for login and leaderboards.

When you sign in using your Google account, Google may collect:

Your Google Play Games profile name

Player ID

Leaderboard scores

Achievement progress

This information is managed by Google and subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not have direct access to your Google account password or sensitive personal data.

3. Advertising (Unity Ads)

The Game uses Unity Ads to display advertisements.

Unity Ads may collect:

Device information

Advertising ID

IP address

Interaction data with advertisements

Approximate location (non-precise)

This information is used to provide relevant advertisements and measure ad performance.

For more information, please review Unity's Privacy Policy.

You can limit ad personalization through your device settings.

4. Analytics and Performance Data

Third-party services may automatically collect:

Device type and operating system

App version

Gameplay statistics

Crash reports

Usage patterns

This data is used to improve performance, fix bugs, and enhance user experience.

5. Data Storage

Game progress is stored locally on your device.

Leaderboard data is stored through Google Play Games Services.

6. Children's Privacy

Fandom Frenzy is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Fan-Made Content Disclaimer

Fandom Frenzy is a fan-made app created for entertainment purposes. All trademarks, names, and references to music groups, dramas, actors, or entertainment properties belong to their respective owners.

No copyright infringement is intended.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" above.
